About Me

August 16, 2026

The older I get, the more curious I am about how things work and they came to be.

In 2023, I experienced my first layoff. My career hasn't been the same since and that change has been scary.

But change is always scary and my first line of defense is deconstruction. How did I get here? What are the variables I can control?

The only thing you can control on this Earth are you and your actions. I choose to always learn.

This blog is my first love letter to myself: a place to document my learnings and adapt what I learn to fit different mediums. This helps me become a better writer and translator. This site is my digital portfolio and my indirect way of turning life’s unexpected changes into level-up mechanisms.

Thanks for joining me on this ride as I take back my life, my finances, and my spirit.
