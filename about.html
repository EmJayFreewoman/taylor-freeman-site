About Me

August 16, 2026

If I don’t have a goal to work toward, it’s really difficult for me to stay motivated. I’m also incredibly competitive—not something I’m proud of—but my biggest competitor is myself.

In 2023, I experienced my first layoff. I was a community manager at Red Hat, managing blog curation for an enterprise architect subsite. I’d just bought my first home, and I didn’t know how to feel. I was fortunate to land a new job almost immediately: office admin for an architect leader I’d worked with at Red Hat, building an APM practice across Danaher. I’ll be forever grateful for that experience, but it wasn’t stress-free. I won’t self-deprecate; the point of this blog is to challenge old thinking patterns, not berate myself. So I’ll just say it: I bombed the hell out of that role.

Now that elephant’s out of the way, I’ll add that I bombed the next role even harder. Two months after the Danaher contract ended, I landed the highest-paying, most senior role of my career to date: senior content marketing manager at Sauce Labs. Nine months in, I was laid off, along with my team. For me, that was the straw that broke the camel’s back—my morale, that is. I was out of work for six months, burning through what was left of my unemployment within months. A temporary part-time gig paid the bills, until those hours got cut and I was back up shit’s creek without a paddle—every dollar going to bills and debt.

Now, the lesson here isn’t “I’m an epic failure” or “my employers sucked.” The lesson is, when you stop pouring into yourself you’ve lost sight of true purpose, like a hamster on a hamster wheel. When the noise of a job finally went silent and I was left with nothing but my thoughts, I realized this was my biggest deficit—not cash flow, not knowledge, not competence, not grit, but my own goals. I don’t even think I knew who I was outside of a job.

This blog is my first love letter to myself, and my commitment to my future. I never want to stop learning and being curious, or become so reliant on a corporation that it’s bankrolling my self-worth because that’s my (our) true job. My goal for this digital portfolio site is to give you insights helpful enough to turn life’s unexpected changes into level-up mechanisms of your own. Thanks for joining me on this ride as I take back my life, my finances, and my spirit.
