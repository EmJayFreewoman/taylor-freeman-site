What’s the purpose of this site?

This blog turns my two layoffs in three years into a practical guide for staying employable in an unpredictable economy. I share what actually closed my skills gap, including some uncomfortable conversations with past employers, so you can shortcut the trial and error and build a skills-based playbook to follow your dreams, get your career back on track, or both.
